# Missing Turing Scholar Profiles — September 29, 2026

Completed at 2026-09-29T09:44:27-04:00 for all 41 Turing Award winners with blank canonical Google Scholar links.
Ran 72 distinct general-web queries in serial groups of at most four, with 15-second pauses or intervening inspection between search calls; the first four-query group was repeated after its initial output was truncated.

All 41 searches are appended to the [search-history CSV](../data/google_scholar_profile_searches.csv), preserving every earlier row.

## Scope and Limits

This is bounded missing-link discovery, not proof that no matching public profiles exist.
No Google Scholar profile was fetched, no publication sample was crawled, and no live availability or quality conclusion is made.
General-web index results and selected non-Scholar source pages were inspected; Scholar keyword searches, paper links, namesakes, collaborators and award names are not author-profile matches.
Previously rejected IDs and unavailable associations remain excluded; indexed directory listings do not override user decisions.
The [September 16 Turing search](turing_scholar_search_2026-09-16.csv), [Turing audit](turing_scholar_audit_2026-09-16.md) and individual prior ledger reports retain earlier findings.
Not-found replacement searches can coexist with a historical rejected or unavailable ID, as noted for Micali, Emerson, Milner, Hoare and Iverson below.
For the seven rows retaining a historical candidate URL, `inaccessible` and `wrong_person` describe preserved dispositions, not a fresh Scholar check.
No actionable candidate or unresolved lead is opened by this batch.
Canonical award links, quality flags, capture dates, extracted JSON data, capture/import queues and visualizations are unchanged.
